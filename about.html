About Me

Hello, I'm Lebron.

Hi! I’m Lebron Carmelo Espeleta, also known as Melo. I’m a first-year AI Engineering student at Mapúa, and honestly? I got interested in AI because of my love for Iron Man and Pokémon. I’m a Pokémon fan at heart, and those games feature all kinds of AI. I want to build something similar someday, and possibly become a machine learning engineer or a game developer.

Why AI Engineering

  1. Future opportunities

    As we all know, AI has been booming and evolving every single day. Because of that, I took the risk and chose this course, even though it is still new and somewhat unknown.

  2. Inspired by Iron Man

    Iron Man was, and still is, a big inspiration for me. Tony Stark is smart and witty, and he makes it look like he has everything in the palm of his hand. I want to reach that level of freedom in the things I do and pursue my passion for technology and AI.
